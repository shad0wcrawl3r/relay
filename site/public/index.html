Desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux

Write it, ship it, tell people.

Relay is a desktop workbench for building in public. Write on Dev.to, promote what you publish on LinkedIn through Buffer, and keep your Plane board and a terminal right beside it.

Download Relay View on GitHub

Free and open source. Your data stays on your machine.

What it does

Write

A markdown editor with preview and image placeholders. Posts go to Dev.to as unpublished drafts; publishing is a separate, confirmed step.

Edited a draft on Dev.to? Relay notices and shows the difference before it would overwrite anything.

Promote

Turn a published post into a LinkedIn draft, by hand or with an AI model you choose (local models work). You review every word before it goes to Buffer, and the article's link is always attached.

Buffer, in full

Your channels, posting schedules and goals; the queue, drafts, approvals, sent and failed posts. Edit, reschedule and delete. Capture ideas, drafted with AI from anything you paste.

Plane boards

A fast kanban for your Plane workspace, with an attention inbox and a Buffer column: drag a work item in to save it as an idea, or an idea out to make it a work item. Plane is optional.

Terminal

Sessions that keep running in the background, with tabs, splits, zoom and tmux-style keys. A quick terminal (⌘/) drops over any view.

Private by default

Everything Relay stores is encrypted, with the key and your tokens in the system keychain. Lock projects behind Touch ID. No accounts, no analytics.

Builds aren't signed yet. How to open Relay the first time

macOS (Apple silicon)

  1. Unzip and move relay.app to Applications.
  2. Open it once. macOS says it can't check it for malware; click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Relay.

Or in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/relay.app

Windows

  1. Run the installer.
  2. If SmartScreen says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.

Linux

  1. Debian or Ubuntu: sudo apt install ./relay.deb
  2. Other distributions: extract the .tar.gz; Relay needs GTK 4 and WebKitGTK 6.

To check a download is the file CI built, compare it with the release's SHA256SUMS: shasum -a 256 <file> on macOS, sha256sum <file> on Linux, Get-FileHash <file> in PowerShell.

Privacy

  • Stays on your machine. Posts, settings and caches are stored locally and encrypted (AES-256-GCM). The key, and every token and API key, live in your system keychain.
  • Goes only where you connect it. Relay talks to Plane, Dev.to, Buffer and the AI provider you pick, and nothing else. There's no Relay server, account or telemetry.
  • AI only when you ask. An article reaches a model provider only when you ask for a summary. Local models (Ollama, LM Studio) keep it on your machine.
  • Private projects stay private. Work from a locked project only goes to a private provider: a model you run or control, marked as such in Settings. Unlocking a project doesn't change that.
  • Sign-in passes through, nothing kept. Buffer needs an https address to return to after sign-in, so it comes to this site's /oauth/callback, which hands the result straight to Relay on your machine. It stores nothing and loads no third-party code.
